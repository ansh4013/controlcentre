How the score works
  • Reach – lifetime views on a log scale, so one huge site doesn't flatten the rest.
  • Traction – moving average of the last 14 days; recent days count more.
  • Momentum – this week vs last week, smoothed. 50 means flat.
  • Consistency – recency-weighted active days, penalising volatile spikes.
  • Engagement – Wilson 95% bound (Reddit "best") of visits that stay 30s+, then an IMDb-style Bayesian average.
  • Authority – PageRank (Google) over how your sites link to each other.
  • Freshness – Hacker-News-style gravity: a view today is worth far more than a week ago.
  • Elo – daily head-to-head matches with margin-of-victory scaling, like chess and FiveThirtyEight ratings.
  • Rank fusion – merges each algorithm's ranking (Reciprocal Rank Fusion, as in search engines); robust to outliers.
  • Confidence – sites with few views are damped toward the field average and can't be boosted by luck.
